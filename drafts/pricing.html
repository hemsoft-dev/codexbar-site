Pricing

CodexBar for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is free to download. CodexBar Pro unlocks every provider, account and feature.

  • Free

    • One provider and one account
    • Dashboard
    • 7 days of history

  • CodexBar Pro

    • Unlimited providers and accounts
    • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
    • The Apple Watch app
    • Full history
    • Usage alerts

CodexBar Pro prices

  • Monthly: US$1.99 per month
  • Yearly: US$14.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial
  • Lifetime: US$29.99, paid once

These are US prices. Prices vary by country or region, and the App Store shows your local price before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Bought CodexBar before?

If you bought the paid CodexBar app, you get lifetime CodexBar Pro at no extra cost.
