What CodexBar does

Connect the providers you use and CodexBar keeps their numbers on one dashboard, whether you use one provider or several.

  • Limits, balances and resets

    Usage limits, balances, reset times and refresh status, wherever a provider makes those numbers available.

  • Several accounts

    Multiple accounts and provider groups keep work and personal setups apart.

  • History and alerts

    Up to 90 days of daily usage history with charts, plus usage alerts for the metrics that matter to you.

  • Widgets and Apple Watch

    Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and an Apple Watch dashboard with complications. Setup stays on the iPhone.

  • Your dashboard, your way

    Show, hide, reorder, resize and restyle individual metrics.

  • Private by design

    No CodexBar account. Credentials stay on your device, and requests go straight to the providers you connect, not through a HemSoft server.

Supported providers

CodexBar for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch supports:

  • ChatGPT / Codex
  • GitHub Copilot
  • GitHub Billing
  • Claude
  • Grok
  • X Subscription
  • Cursor
  • OpenRouter
  • OpenCode Go + Zen
  • Moonshot (Kimi)
  • Greptile
  • Google Gemini

Available metrics and sign-in methods depend on the service, your account, your subscription and its permissions. Data a provider doesn't return stays unavailable instead of being guessed. The Windows and macOS apps support different sets of providers; see each project's README.
